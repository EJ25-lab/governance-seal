That’s the gap we close — the first accredited, standardized program for how a school board governs.

⚠ Open work — the honest part

The ramp needs substance. This is ours to build.

The model shows 8 → 20 → 40 → 70 paying boards a year once accredited. That’s a direction, not yet a plan — and it’s the biggest thing we owe ourselves real answers on:

  • How do we actually close boards? The capacity underneath the ramp — who sells, at what close rate, how many build-days a year we can staff.
  • How does this become a credential communities ask for? Boards are elected — adoption follows public demand. Making the Seal nationally desired is the growth work, not an afterthought.
  • Which capital fits? Whether this grows as a focused services business or reaches for something faster — and what that asks of us.

These are the questions on the board — bring your sharpest thinking.
